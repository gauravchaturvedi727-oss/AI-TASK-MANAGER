LifeOS AI is a personal productivity assistant.

Users can create tasks with a title and description.

Users can mark tasks as completed.

LifeOS AI can help users organize their daily tasks and notes.

RAG stands for Retrieval Augmented Generation.

RAG retrieves relevant information from a knowledge base before asking an LLM to generate an answer.

Embeddings represent text as numerical vectors.

FAISS is used to search vectors efficiently and find information that is semantically similar to a user's question.

Qwen is the language model used by LifeOS AI to generate answers.
